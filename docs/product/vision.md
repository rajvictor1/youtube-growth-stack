# Product vision

YouTube Growth Stack is an open-source, voice-first research agent for creators.

The creator should be able to say:

> Find the strongest competitor outliers in my niche, explain what they have in common, and give me ten ideas grounded in the evidence.

The agent gathers source data, runs analysis, speaks back a concise answer, and places detailed evidence in a visual workspace.

## Product principles

1. Voice controls the work; the canvas verifies it.
2. Evidence comes before generation.
3. A tool result is never overstated.
4. Long work is durable and observable.
5. Paid and mutating actions require approval.
6. Text remains available for URLs, names, accessibility, and precision.

## Initial user

An independent creator or small content team that manually studies YouTube competitors and wants a faster, repeatable research workflow.

## Core loop

1. Create a project and describe the niche.
2. Add competitor channels or a research question.
3. Collect YouTube data and optional enrichment.
4. Find outliers, patterns, and content gaps.
5. Generate ideas with source evidence.
6. Save ideas, publish videos, and later import results.
